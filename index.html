Why speed-to-lead matters

Portal buyers usually enquire on several listings at once. The agent who speaks to them first tends to get the viewing. Most teams can't call back within minutes at 11pm or on a Sunday, which is where Mergro comes in: the first call happens while the buyer is still on the listing page.

What happens when a lead comes in

  1. Instant call. A new inquiry from Zillow, Rightmove, Bayut or a website form triggers a call within about a minute.
  2. Qualification. The agent asks about budget, timeline and preferred areas, then pulls those out into structured fields.
  3. Booking. Qualified buyers are booked straight into a viewing slot.
  4. CRM logging. A call summary, transcript and extracted details are written back to your CRM.
  5. Follow-up. Leads who don't pick up are followed up by phone, WhatsApp, email, SMS or website chat.
  6. Re-engagement. Old and cold leads in your database can be called again to surface buyers who are ready now.
